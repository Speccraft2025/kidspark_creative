FAQs

A few common questions

Do you ship physical products?

Right now our packs are digital downloads you can print at home. Physical storybooks and merchandise are part of our roadmap — get in touch if you'd like early access.

How can my school or NGO use Kidspark content?

We offer bulk licensing and custom packs for classrooms, libraries, and community programs at affordable rates. Reach out and tell us about your group.

Can I license Kidspark characters for my brand?

Yes — we're open to thoughtful licensing and brand collaborations that align with our mission of joyful, safe, educational content for children.

How do I stay updated on new releases?

Join the Spark List from our homepage or shop page — we'll email you the moment new packs and stories drop.
